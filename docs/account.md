# Sign in and manage your account

[Español](es/account.md)

Your Meltemi account is used for hosted Diagrams projects and the account workspace. Sign in with
GitHub or Google; there is no separate Meltemi password. Meltemi IDE, local diagramming and reading
the documentation do not require this account.

## Sign in

1. Choose **Sign in** in Diagrams, or open [your account](https://id.meltemi.dev/account?lang=en).
2. Choose **Continue with GitHub** or **Continue with Google**.
3. Complete the provider's sign-in and return to Meltemi.

Use the same provider account when you return. To add another way to sign in to the same Meltemi
account, link it from your account page after signing in.

If a sign-in cannot be confirmed or has expired, start again from Meltemi in the same browser. If
the provider is temporarily unavailable, retry later. If Meltemi reports that an account with
your email already exists, follow that page's instructions to return to your original account;
do not assume that signing in with another provider always joins accounts.

## Link or unlink a sign-in

Open [Sign-ins and sessions](https://id.meltemi.dev/account?lang=en).

- Under **Link another provider**, choose **Link GitHub** or **Link Google** and complete that
  provider's sign-in.
- Confirm that the provider appears under **Linked sign-ins**.
- To remove one, choose **Unlink GitHub** or **Unlink Google** on its row. Keep at least one
  linked provider: Meltemi refuses to unlink your only sign-in.

A provider account that is already linked to a different Meltemi account cannot be added to this
one. Use the account you intended to keep before making further changes.

## Check devices and sign out

In **Where you are signed in**, review the device labels and their last activity. **This
session** identifies the browser you are using.

- Choose **Sign out** on another device's row to end that session.
- Choose **Sign out of other devices** to end every other session while staying signed in here.
- To leave this browser, choose **Sign out** for the current session or in the account menu,
  then confirm on the sign-out page. Signing out keeps your account and projects.

A product can take up to an hour to recognize a session you have ended. If a device is
unfamiliar, end its session and review access to the GitHub or Google account you use to sign in.

## Change your profile and preferences

| Page                                                              | What to do                                                                                                                             |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Profile](https://id.meltemi.dev/account/profile?lang=en)         | Enter your preferred display name and choose **Save profile**. Leave it empty to use your provider's name. The limit is 80 characters. |
| [Preferences](https://id.meltemi.dev/account/preferences?lang=en) | Choose appearance and preferred language, then choose **Save preferences**.                                                            |

These preferences apply to your account and administration workspace. Device appearance and
browser language are available as automatic choices. An explicit page language takes precedence
over the saved preference. The documentation has its own language and appearance controls;
diagram editor options stay in the editor, and IDE settings stay on your machine.

## Delete your account

Account deletion cannot be undone. Save anything you need from your hosted projects first.

1. Open [Sign-ins and sessions](https://id.meltemi.dev/account?lang=en).
2. Find **Delete your account**.
3. Type the confirmation word shown on the page and choose **Delete my account**.

This removes your Meltemi account, linked sign-ins and sessions. Meltemi products delete the data
they hold about you within seven days. It does not delete your GitHub or Google account or local
files on your machine.

For the hosted services' data handling, read the
[privacy notice](https://meltemi.dev/privacy/).
